Word Embeddings: Domain-Specific vs. Pretrained Models

Problem
Semantic similarity can reflect domain context or shared spelling, making useful text representations difficult to select.
Approach
Trained four Word2Vec and FastText models using CBOW and skip-gram on 2,000 movie reviews, then compared selected neighbors with pretrained Google News embeddings.
Impact
Illustrated domain-specific associations and subword effects, showing why larger cosine scores alone do not establish better semantic relevance. Original results require validation after resolving training warnings.
